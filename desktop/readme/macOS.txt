Ash Mountain Shrine (prototype) for macOS
The app is not signed by Apple, so macOS will block it at first. After unzipping, open Terminal and run:
  xattr -cr "/path/to/Ash Mountain Shrine.app"
(drag the app onto the Terminal window to fill in the path), then right-click the app and choose Open.
Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later) use the arm64 build; older Intel Macs use the x64 build.
Runs fully offline.
