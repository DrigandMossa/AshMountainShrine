    The shrine bowl

    It is very quiet. Something inside is listening.

      Trade

      Chapter two: her house

      She gave you an axe that never tires. Now she wants her shrine made whole, and she will be watching you from the trees.
      • Restore the shrine in three steps. Each step needs wood, coins and a lost artifact. Check the list in your panel.
      • The signpost by the peddler now leads down to the village. You can buy and find artifacts there.
      • Some figures in the village are not people. Don't look straight at them. If the villagers see you staring at nothing, their suspicion rises, and when it fills they chase you out until morning.
      • Your wisplings still need food, beds and uppies while you're away.

      Chapter three: a family house

      Her shrine is whole. She doesn't hide from you anymore. She walks beside you, and the little ones need a bigger home.
      • Grow your cabin into a shataku, one big house for everyone, in three steps. Start at the plan board by your cabin.
      • You'll need house plans. Tokubei, the old carpenter in the village, has some, if you can help him first.
      • Oharu the weaver can make a red thread charm. With it, carry a wispling to the village path and they'll come with you.
      • Tap her to talk. She has a lot to tell you.

      Ash Mountain Shrine

      The village cast you out for seeing spirits. Up here, the old shrine sees you back. She was their offering once. Now she wants her children back.
      • You see through your own eyes. Walk with WASD, hold Shift to run, jump with Space, look with the mouse (click to grab it, hold Alt to let go and click buttons, Esc to pause, save or load) or turn with the arrow keys. On a phone, drag the left side to walk (push all the way to run), the right side to look, and tap Jump to hop.
      • Look at something and click or press E to chop, pick berries, cook or build. Tap far things to walk to them.
      • Sell wood at the peddler's stump for coins. Cook food into treats at the campfire.
      • Offer coins and treats at the shrine. Enough of both wakes a wispling.
      • Give wisplings jobs, keep the pantry stocked, and build each pair a hut.
      • Mend your cabin, then step inside. Build a kitchen (set wisplings to Cook treats) and a dinner table.
      • Once the table is built, the wisplings eat there once a day at sunset. They gather and drum on the table until you serve them, 1 food each.
      • Tap a wispling (or press E) to pick them up. When one asks for uppies, go carry them before she does. While holding one, press Q or right-click (or tap Toss) to throw them up and catch them.
      • Bring her something every day. If her wrath fills, the mountain wakes. Wake five wisplings and give each a bed to finish the first chapter.
      • Choose to play as a woman or a man below, and change your eyes, mouth, hair and clothes. You can change them later from the ☺ menu.

      Chapter four: her memory

      Your house is whole, and so is her shrine. The mountain’s spirit is strong enough now to raise a barrier: no evil spirit can climb up here anymore.
      • Rin moves into your house. Build her room if you haven’t yet. At sunset she comes inside and feeds the wisplings herself.
      • Finish both wispling bedrooms. When the little ones move in, their old huts won’t stay empty for long.
      • After she has served them supper, her mirror on the shrine will start to glow. Look into it.
